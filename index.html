Thanh-Dat Truong

Postdoctoral Fellow · University of Arkansas

Thanh-Dat Truong’s research focuses on robust and fair vision learning, including large multimodal learning, adaptation learning, continual learning, and video understanding.

Email Google Scholar GitHub Twitter

He received his PhD in Computer Science at the University of Arkansas in 2024, where he was supervised by Dr. Khoa Luu. He received his B.Sc. degree in Computer Science from the Honors Program, University of Science, VNU in 2019 under the supervision of Dr. Khoa Luu and Dr. Minh-Triet Tran. He was a research intern with Dr. Minh Do at Coordinated Science Laboratory at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign in 2018.
